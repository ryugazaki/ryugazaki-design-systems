Wheel basics

  • 3 hours
  • Up to 8 people
  • No experience needed

You will center, open, and pull two cylinders on your own wheel. We trim and bisque them, you come back to glaze, and they are ready about three weeks later.

Pick a date

Thursday evenings and Saturdays to the end of October.

Pick a time on Sat 24 Oct

Up to 4 for this session. Bigger group? Ask about a private class.

Before you come

What should I wear?

Clothes that can take a splash of slip, and closed shoes. Clay washes out of cotton easily. Short nails make centering much easier.

Can I come on my own?

Most people do. Everyone gets their own wheel, and the table in the middle is where conversations start.

When can I collect my pieces?

About three weeks after your class. We text you when they leave the glaze kiln, and they wait on your shelf for a month.

What if I need to cancel?

Move or cancel for free up to 48 hours before. After that we can move you to another date once.
