How a night works

From a forecast at five to a hot drink at one.

  1. 17:00

    We pick the route

    The guide on duty compares three cloud models and chooses between the coast, the Lyngen valley, and the Finnish border. You get a text with the plan.

  2. 20:40

    Pickup at your hotel

    Thermal suits, boots, and mittens are in the bus. Drives run from 40 minutes to two hours, depending on where the gap in the clouds is.

  3. 01:30

    Photos in your inbox

    Your guide shoots on a full frame camera all night. The edited set arrives before breakfast, free.

Questions before you book

What happens if the forecast turns bad?

If we cancel at 17:00, you get a full refund or a seat on another night, your choice. If we go out and the sky stays empty, you get a free seat on a later tour.

How cold does it get?

Between minus 5 and minus 25 degrees inland. The suits we lend are rated to minus 30. Wear wool under them, not cotton.

Can children come?

Yes, from age 6 on the cabin tour and age 10 on the minibus chase. Children ride at half price.
