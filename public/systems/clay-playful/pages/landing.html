How a lesson works

Listen, squish, read it back.

Six to nine minutes, short enough for the gap between dinner and bath.

  1. 1

    Hear the story

    A narrator reads each page while the words light up. Any word can be tapped to hear it alone.

  2. 2

    Squish the sounds

    Push letter tiles together: f, o, x. The sounds blend as the tiles touch.

  3. 3

    Read it back

    Your child reads one page out loud to you. No microphone grading and no scores; you tap the star.
