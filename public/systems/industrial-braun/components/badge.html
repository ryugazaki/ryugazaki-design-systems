key

on
Success. The sensor reported in the last two minutes.
standby
Neutral. Paired and asleep until the next report.
offline
No report for ten minutes. Check the battery.
fault
Danger. The reading is out of range or the sensor failed a self test.
fw
Firmware version, a plain panel label.
now playing
The single current item. The only orange on the screen.
